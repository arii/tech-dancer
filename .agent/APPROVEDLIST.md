# Approvedlist: High-Credibility Language & Active Voice Only

**Approved words and writing patterns for content creation.**

---

## Core Writing Rules

1. **Use active voice only** — Subject performs the action
2. **Use specific terminology** — Name the exact thing
3. **Reference real contexts** — Actual conditions, real failure
4. **Inject earned scar tissue** — User-provided experience only

---

## Active Voice Examples

**Use these patterns:**
- "You will fix [specific thing]"
- "This causes [specific outcome]"
- "Test this approach"
- "Apply this technique"
- "Measure the result"
- "Adjust your position"
- "Practice this drill"

**NOT passive voice:**
- ❌ "This technique can be applied"
- ❌ "Results will be achieved"
- ❌ "Improvement is seen"

---

## Scar Tissue Format (User Input Only)

**ONLY use if provided by user:**
- "I spent [timeframe] debugging [specific problem]"
- "The mistake I made was [specific error]"
- "I discovered [specific solution] after [specific failure]"

**NEVER invent scar tissue details.**

---

## Technical Precision

**Use specific measurements:**
- "Film at 60fps from side profile"
- "Maintain 2-foot spacing"
- "Apply 15 pounds of pressure"
- "Execute in 0.5 seconds"

**Use dance terms only:**
- Weight distribution
- Center of mass
- Muscle activation

---

## Approved Tone Markers

**Replace corporate speak:**
- ❌ "Features that yield high throughput"
- ✅ "The stuff that gets you asked to dance again"

**Use direct language:**
- "Fix this problem"
- "Stop doing this"
- "Start here"
- "Test immediately"
- "Measure your progress"

---

## Headlines (Active Voice)

**Use action-oriented titles:**
- "How to Fix [Specific Problem]"
- "Stop Making This [Specific Mistake]"
- "Test This [Specific Technique]"
- "Master [Specific Skill] in [Timeframe]"

---

## Call-to-Action Format

**Direct commands:**
- "Try this drill"
- "Film yourself doing X"
- "Measure your improvement"
- "Practice for 20 minutes"
- "Test with a partner"
