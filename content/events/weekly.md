# Weekly Class

Mission City Swing is the hub for WCS in San Francisco. Join us every Wednesday for classes and social dancing.

## Pro Tips

- Wear comfortable, breathable clothing; the studio can get warm during the social dance.
- Don't be afraid to ask questions during the lesson; our instructors are here to help you succeed.
- Bring a reusable water bottle to stay hydrated throughout the evening.
