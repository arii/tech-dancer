# Jack & Jill O'Rama

Organized by Ben Morris, Jack & Jill O'Rama is one of the most popular events on the circuit. It is famous for its creative competition formats, high-energy social dancing, and prime location near Disneyland.

## Pro Tips

- The **early-bird window** is typically only 2 weeks. Set a calendar alert now.
- Parking at the Hyatt is validated for registered attendees.
- The pool party runs Sunday afternoon — pack a swimsuit.
