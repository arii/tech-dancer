# Boogie by the Bay

The flagship event of The Next Generation Swing Dance Club, held every Columbus Day weekend. Boogie by the Bay features the Champions/All-Star Jack & Jill, professional intensives, and a massive ballroom floor.

## Pro Tips

- The Hyatt Regency SFO is huge; give yourself extra time to get from your room to the ballroom.
- The Sunday night social is legendary—don't plan an early flight home on Monday!
- Burlingame has great dining options just a short shuttle ride away if you need a break from hotel food.
