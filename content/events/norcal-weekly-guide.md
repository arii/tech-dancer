Northern California is one of the most active hubs for West Coast Swing in the world. If you're looking to dance during the week, here are the anchor locations for the NorCal community.

### Weekly Socials & Classes

#### Mission City Swing (San Jose)
- **When:** Every Thursday Night
- **Where:** St. Leo the Great School, San Jose, CA
- **Details:** The South Bay's primary WCS club. Offers three levels of classes and a high-energy social. [Official Site](http://missioncityswing.com/)

#### Next Generation Swing Dance Club (San Francisco/San Mateo)
- **When:** Tuesdays
- **Details:** One of the longest-running WCS clubs in the country. Known for great workshops and a welcoming atmosphere for all ages. [Official Site](https://nextgenswing.com/)

#### Sacramento Westie Socials
- **When:** Varies (Check the [Sacramento WCS Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/sacwcs/) for the latest)
- **Details:** A growing community in the Central Valley with regular workshops and social dances.

### Regional Directory Resources

For a complete and up-to-date calendar of all NorCal WCS events, we recommend checking these community-maintained resources:

1. **[World Swing Dance Council (WSDC) Registry](https://www.worldsdc.com/events/):** For a list of all sanctioned weekend events globally.
2. **[West Coast Swing NorCal Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/WCSNorCal/):** The best place to find last-minute class updates and local social announcements.

### New to NorCal?
If you're visiting the area, we recommend starting with [Mission City Swing](/events/mission-city-swing) on a Thursday. It's centrally located in the South Bay and has a very friendly "newcomer" vibe.
