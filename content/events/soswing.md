# SOswing

A charming West Coast Swing experience in the heart of Southern Oregon. SOswing offers a unique, community-focused atmosphere in beautiful Ashland. Known for its 'Westie' hospitality, the event features leveled workshops and late-night social dancing.

## Pro Tips

- Ashland is a theatre town — beautiful, small, and surprisingly walkable from the hotel.
- Book early; the room block is limited and tends to fill up quickly with returning attendees.
- Take some time to explore the local wineries and hiking trails if you can extend your stay.
