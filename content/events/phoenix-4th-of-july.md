# Phoenix 4th of July

Celebrate Independence Day at one of the most luxurious resorts on the swing circuit. Known for the 'Paradise Ballroom' and the legendary All-Star Pool Party, Phoenix 4th of July combines a high-level WSDC competition with a luxury vacation vibe.

## Pro Tips

- Hydration is key in the Arizona heat—bring plenty of water and electrolytes.
- The pool party is a major highlight; make sure to pack your favorite swimwear and sunscreen.
- The resort is sprawling, so bring comfortable shoes for walking between the ballroom and the amenities.
