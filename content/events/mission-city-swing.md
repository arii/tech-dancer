# Mission City Swing

Weekly social dance in San Jose. Mission City Swing is a weekly social dance event in San Jose, California, featuring West Coast Swing. It's a great opportunity to practice, learn, and connect with the local dance community.

## Pro Tips

- Arrive early for the beginner lesson to help welcome new dancers to the community.
- There's plenty of free parking in the lot behind the venue.
- Stay late for the social dance; the music is always fantastic and the energy stays high until the end.
