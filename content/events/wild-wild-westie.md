# Wild Wild Westie

Dallas event. Wild Wild Westie is a premier WCS event in Dallas, known for its high energy and competitive atmosphere.

## Pro Tips

- Book the hotel room block immediately as it fills fast—sometimes within hours of opening!
- Dallas in July is hot, but the ballroom is heavily air-conditioned; dress in layers.
- Take advantage of the late-night social dancing; some of the best dances happen in the early hours of the morning.
