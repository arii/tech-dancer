Halloween is one of the best times of year for social dancing, but nothing ruins a dance faster than a costume that gets in the way. Whether you're planning for a local social or a major event like Boogie by the Bay, choosing the right outfit is critical.

### Costume safety checklist

When selecting or building your costume, run through this checklist to ensure you (and your partner) stay safe on the floor:

- **Arms & Shoulders:** Can you raise your arms for a sugar tuck without the costume riding up or restricting movement?
- **Turns & Spins:** Will your costume whip your partner in the face during a spin? Avoid capes or long loose fabric unless they are secured.
- **Floor Visibility:** Ensure you can see your feet and the area around you. Masks should have wide eye-holes.
- **Partner Safety:** Avoid sharp accessories, spikes, or loose sequins that can catch on your partner's hair or clothing.
- **Pockets:** Does your costume have a place for your phone, keys, or fan?
- **Hair & Accessories:** Secure wigs and headpieces firmly. A flying wig is a major trip hazard.

### Easy & Safe Ideas

### Final Tip

Always do a "test dance" at home before the event. If you can't do a full left-side pass comfortably, it's time to adjust the costume!
