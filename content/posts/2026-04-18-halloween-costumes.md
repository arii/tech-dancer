<article-section title="Functional Theming">

Halloween social dancing requires gear that doesn't restrict your frame. You want to stay thematic without sacrificing your connection or movement.

<callout title="Pro Tip" variant="tip">
Always test your costume in a private practice session before hitting the social floor. If you can't hit a triple step or a compression-led anchor, the costume is too restrictive.
</callout>

</article-section>

<article-section title="The Pumpkin Outfit">

I've stress-tested a specific **Pumpkin outfit** that works perfectly for the social floor. It includes a low-profile headband and lightweight stickers.

<pullquote quote="You want to look like a pumpkin but move like a technical dancer." author="Ariel Anders" />

The key is keeping the costume flexible. Avoid anything that restricts the ribcage or the shoulder blades.

</article-section>
