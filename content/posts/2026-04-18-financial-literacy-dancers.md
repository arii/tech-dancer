## The Logistics of WCS Travel

Attending West Coast Swing events is one of the most rewarding parts of the dance lifestyle, but it can also be a significant financial burden. Between event passes, flights, hotels, and workshops, the costs add up quickly. Managing these logistics effectively is what separates the occasional attendee from the sustainable community member.

## Social Dancing and Long-Term Health

It's easy to get "swung over" by the excitement of a 3 AM social set, but a responsible lifestyle means planning for the recovery too.

### Credit Card Strategy

A deep dive into status-stacking with travel cards is a game changer. I use the Amex Platinum and Hyatt cards to secure late checkouts and airport lounge access. This allows for a much more relaxed Monday morning, significantly reducing the impact of post-event fatigue.

### Cost Saving Tips

*   **Early Bird Windows:** Most events offer significant discounts if you register 6+ months in advance.
*   **Group Housing:** Coordinate with friends early to secure quad-occupancy rooms.
*   **Airport Logistics:** Use lounge access to save on expensive airport meals.

By treating your dance journey as a logistics challenge, you can build a sustainable financial foundation that allows for more dancing and less stress.
