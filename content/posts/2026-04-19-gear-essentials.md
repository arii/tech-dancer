## Elevating Your Dance Weekend

We focus on shoes and costumes, but the logistics of a dance weekend can make or break your performance. Here are three items I never travel without:

1. **Loop Earplugs:** Protect your hearing without losing the beat.
2. **Industrial travel steamer:** Keep your clothes from melting or wrinkling and look professional even after a 6-hour flight (Saves 40 minutes of convention prep time).
3. **Portable Speaker:** Don't rely on your phone's tinny speakers for hotel practice.
