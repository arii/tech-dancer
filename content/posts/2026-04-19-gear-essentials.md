Traveling for West Coast Swing events is an art form. You're balancing shoe bags, costume changes, and enough caffeine to survive social dancing until 4 AM. After years of attending events across the country, these are the three items I never leave behind.

### 1. Loop Experience Earplugs
Ballroom acoustics are notoriously difficult. Between the thumping bass and the echoing announcements, your ears take a beating. [Loop Experience Earplugs](/gear/loop-experience-earplugs) reduce the volume without muffling the music, so you can still hear your partner and the beat.

**Best for:** Loud ballrooms and late-night socials.
**Skip if:** You prefer total silence (try Loop Quiet instead).

### 2. Travel Steamer
Nothing ruins a competition look like deep suitcase wrinkles. A compact [travel steamer](/gear/travel-steamer-pro) is far safer for delicate dance fabrics than a hotel iron.

**Best for:** Removing wrinkles from dresses, shirts, and vests.
**Packing note:** Empty the water tank completely before packing to avoid leaks.

### 3. Portable Bluetooth Speaker
Whether it's for a quick warm-up in your hotel room or reviewing a private lesson video, a small speaker is a game-changer. The [UE Wonderboom](/gear/portable-bluetooth-speaker-ue-wonderboom-4) is rugged, waterproof (pool party ready!), and has surprisingly good bass.

**Event use case:** Hotel room practice or cooling down after the ballroom closes.
**Courtesy note:** Keep the volume low to respect your hotel neighbors!
