## The Social Floor Problem: Center of Mass Instability

If you've ever felt like you're "falling" into your partner or losing your anchor at the end of a slot, you don't have a "dance" problem—you have a pitching problem. Most dancers treat balance as a feeling. I treat it as a center-of-mass (CoM) optimization.

## The Protocol: Pitch Calibration

Finding your pitch is the difference between surviving a fast social floor and dominating the Registry Ledger.

### 1. The 60/40 Split
On the standard social floor, your weight should be distributed 60% toward the balls of your feet. This isn't "leaning"; it's a forward-engaged pitch that allows for instantaneous reactive movement.

### 2. Spine Alignment Matrix
Stack your head over your heart, and your heart over your hips. Any deviation in this vertical stack creates a lever arm that forces your core to work 4x harder to maintain stability.

## The Practical ROI
By adjusting your pitch, you naturally engage the **Connection Compression** required for advanced Sugar Pushes. You aren't just dancing; you're calibrating your machine for maximum efficiency.
