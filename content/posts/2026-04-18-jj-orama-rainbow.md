## The Rainbow Connection: NorCal at O'Rama

Jack & Jill O'Rama is one of the most vibrant events on the West Coast Swing circuit. For those of us based in San Francisco, it's a home-turf battle with a colorful twist.

### The Team Theme

NorCal competes as a unified team, and our signature is the **Rainbow Theme**. It’s a visual representation of our diverse and inclusive community. 

### Gear Check: Costumes you can dance in

Preparing for O'Rama requires specific gear. You need outfits that are:
1. **Thematic:** Rainbow-compliant.
2. **Functional:** Lightweight fabrics that breathe during 4-hour social sets.
3. **Optimized:** Halloween-style costumes that don't restrict your rotation.
