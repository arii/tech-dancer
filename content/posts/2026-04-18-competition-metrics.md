<article-section title="A Clearer Look at a Subjective Sport">

In West Coast Swing, we are judged by humans. Humans are great, but results can vary from heat to heat. While one event result only tells part of the story, looking at scores across multiple events can reveal helpful patterns about how you're progressing and how different panels see your dancing.

<pullquote quote="The goal of this project isn't to rank dancers, but to provide tools for self-improvement." author="Ariel Anders" />

I am excited to announce the development of the **WCS Competition Data Scraper**, a tool designed to make competition results easier to understand.

</article-section>

<article-section title="Core Philosophy">

<callout title="Data Ethics" variant="info">
We prioritize privacy and respectful data collection. No individual dancer names are stored in our public datasets.
</callout>

The tool is built to be a good citizen of the web. We only use public competition data that has already been published. All raw data is processed securely and we don't keep individual records after we've looked at the big picture.

</article-section>

<article-section title="What the tool will show">

Once launched, the lab will feature judging consistency metrics and comparative performance analysis.

- **Judging Variance:** See how consistent judging is across different panels.
- **Relative Performance:** Look at how you performed relative to the overall strength of the heat.

_Stay tuned for the official release in the DevAI Portfolio._

</article-section>
