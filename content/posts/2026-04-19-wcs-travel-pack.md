## The Ultimate WCS Event Checklist

Whether you're heading to a local convention or flying across the country, forgetting a key piece of gear can throw off your entire weekend. This checklist covers everything you need to stay comfortable, look sharp, and keep dancing until the sunrise sets.

### 1. Dance Shoes & Sole Care
*   **Primary Shoes:** Bring your most reliable pair for the main competitions.
*   **Backup Shoes:** A different heel height or sole friction can save your feet if the floor is too sticky or too slick.
*   **Suede Brush:** Essential for maintaining grip on varied hotel ballroom floors.
*   **Shoe Fresheners:** Cedar inserts or charcoal bags help manage moisture between sessions.

### 2. Hearing Protection
*   **High-Fidelity Earplugs (e.g., Loop):** Ballroom sound systems are often tuned for volume. Protect your hearing while still being able to hear the "one" and carry on a conversation.

### 3. Wrinkle Management
*   **Travel Steamer:** Convention life means living out of a suitcase. A compact, powerful steamer ensures your competition vests and dresses look professional without the risk of using a questionable hotel iron.

### 4. Tech & Hotel Room Prep
*   **Portable Charger:** Outlets are rare in ballroom hallways. Keep your phone alive for recording workshops and checking heat lists.
*   **Practice Speaker:** Don't rely on phone speakers for room practice. A small Bluetooth speaker is better for drilling timing and musicality.
*   **Multi-plug Power Strip:** Hotel rooms never have enough outlets for all your devices.

### 5. Recovery & Hygiene
*   **Portable Massage Tool:** A lacrosse ball or mini foam roller can work wonders on sore calves after a long night of social dancing.
*   **Electrolytes:** Staying hydrated is about more than just water.
*   **Personal Hygiene Kit:** Breath mints, extra deodorant, and a small towel are courtesy must-haves for your partners.

### The "Don't Forget" List
*   **Safety Pins:** For emergency costume repairs or stubborn competition bibs.
*   **Extra Socks/Tights:** Always pack one more pair than you think you need.
*   **Pain Relief:** Anti-inflammatories for those inevitable aches.

Packing right means you can spend less time stressing about your gear and more time connecting on the dance floor. See you at the next event!
