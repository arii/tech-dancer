## Understanding WSDC Points Tier Calculation

For many competitive West Coast Swing dancers, understanding the World Swing Dance Council (WSDC) points system is a crucial part of navigating Jack & Jill competitions. Whether you're a Newcomer looking to move up, or a seasoned Advanced dancer eyeing the All-Star division, mastering the **WSDC points tier calculation** is key.

The WSDC utilizes a tier system based on the size of the competition. Simply put, the more competitors in your division, the more points are awarded to the top placements.

### How WSDC Scoring Works in Prelims

Before you can earn points, you have to make the finals. But **how WSDC scoring works** during preliminary and semi-final rounds can sometimes feel like a mystery.

In prelims, judges use a callback system. They evaluate dancers based on timing, technique, and teamwork (often referred to as the 3 T's). Instead of assigning a numerical score, judges simply say "Yes", "Alternate" (sometimes used), or "No" for each competitor. If you get enough "Yes" votes, you move on to the next round.

### Strategies for Making Novice WCS Finals

**Making Novice WCS finals** is a common hurdle. The Novice division is often the largest, meaning the competition is fierce.

Here are a few strategies to help you stand out to the judges and make it to the finals:

1. **Master Your Basics:** Flashy moves won't save poor timing or connection. Solid fundamentals are the most important criteria for judges in Novice.
2. **Prioritize Connection:** West Coast Swing is a conversation. Listen to your partner and adapt to their skill level and style.
3. **Dance to the Music:** Show the judges you are listening to the song. Hitting a break or phrasing your basics to the music demonstrates musicality.
4. **Maintain a Positive Attitude:** Smile and look like you're having fun! A positive, engaging presence can make a difference in a crowded heat.

### Calculating Your Points

Once you make the finals, your placement determines how many points you earn, based on the tier size of the competition.

For the most up-to-date rules on points tier calculation and division rules, always refer to the official WSDC rules document. Tracking your points will help you understand when you are eligible (or required) to move to the next division.
