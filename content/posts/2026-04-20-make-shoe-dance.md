If you have a pair of sneakers that are perfectly broken in but too grippy for the dance floor, don't retire them! With a little effort and about $20 in materials, you can convert them into excellent practice shoes.

### Materials list
- **Adhesive Suede Sheets:** [Suede stick-on sheets](/gear/how-to-suede-your-own-dance-shoes) are the easiest way to go.
- **Heavy-duty Scissors:** For trimming the suede.
- **Isopropyl Alcohol:** To clean the shoe soles.
- **Pencil/Marker:** To trace the shoe outline.

### Step-by-step instructions
1. **Clean the Soles:** Use alcohol to remove any dirt or oils from the bottom of your shoes. This ensures the adhesive bonds properly.
2. **Trace the Outline:** Place your shoe on the back (paper side) of the suede sheet and trace around the sole.
3. **Cut the Suede:** Cut just inside the line you traced for a clean fit. You can choose to cover the [full sole](/gear/how-to-suede-your-own-dance-shoes) or just the ball of the foot.
4. **Peel and Stick:** Align the suede carefully and press firmly.
5. **Set the Bond:** Let the shoes sit for 24 hours before dancing in them to allow the adhesive to fully cure.

### Warnings & Caveats
- **Floor Compatibility:** Suede is for indoor dance floors only. Wearing these outside will ruin the suede and pick up debris that can damage the dance floor.
- **Test Slowly:** DIY suede might have different friction than professional dance shoes. Take a few slow practice turns before going full speed.
- **Damage Risk:** Removing adhesive suede later can be difficult and may leave residue or damage certain shoe materials.
- **Venue Rules:** Some venues have strict rules about "home-made" dance shoes if they leave marks. Always check if you're unsure.
