# BANLIST.md

This file contains words and phrases that are explicitly **prohibited** in content created for BoomTick.Blog.

Do not use these words, especially AI-isms that sound unnatural or overly formal for the community.

## Banned Words / AI-isms
- tapestry
- game-changer
- paradigm shift
- delve
- leverage (as a verb outside of physical/biomechanical context)
- testament
- myriad
- plethora
- utilize (use "use" instead)
- navigate (when referring to abstract concepts, e.g., "navigate the intricacies")
- seamless
- unleash
- unlock
- elevate
- cutting-edge
